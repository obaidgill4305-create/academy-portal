About Us

A school that grows with your child

Fellowship Academy runs a complete academic programme from Nursery through Class 12, following a structured, exam-focused curriculum with small class sizes and close attention to every student.

Alongside regular studies, our Skill Classes give students (and working adults) a practical edge — from Artificial Intelligence and computer literacy to web development, design and freelancing — so learning translates directly into real opportunities.
